- Branches: `core/dsp-engine`, `core/oversampling-engine`, `core/calibration-system`.
- Focus: Build the foundational framework.

### Milestone 2: Core Modeling Engines
- Branches: `dsp/transformer-model`, `dsp/fet-engine`, `dsp/wdf-filter-framework`.

### Milestone 3: First Plugins
- Branches: `plugin/phantom-console`, `plugin/phantom-fet`, `plugin/phantom-tube-eq`.
- Focus: Integrating core and dsp into products.

### Milestone 4: AI Assistance
- Branches: `ai/audio-analysis-engine`, `ai/mix-assistant`.
- Focus: Adding the intelligence layer.
